05 / FAQ

Common questions

How do engagements work?

Every project starts with a free 30–60 minute discovery call. If we’re a fit, you get a written proposal with scope, deliverables, milestones, timeline, and a fixed price — you sign off before any code is written. During the build you get weekly updates and working code at every milestone.

Who actually does the work?

I do. No subcontractors, no account managers, no relay phone-tag — you talk directly to the engineer writing the code.

What timezone overlap can I expect?

I’m in Phnom Penh (UTC+7). My morning overlaps with East Asia and Australia, my afternoon with European mornings, and for the Americas I schedule calls in my evening. Async-first, with real-time overlap where it matters.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes — happy to sign yours before we discuss specifics. Most of my production work is proprietary, so working under confidentiality is the norm, not the exception.

What happens after handover?

You get documentation, deployment, and knowledge transfer to your team, plus an optional follow-up support window. The goal is that your team owns the system — not that you depend on me forever.
